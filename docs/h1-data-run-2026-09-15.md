## Findings for the pre-registration

1. **Arm H cannot discriminate inside a subsystem-matched pair.** Its verdict is a
   property of the subsystem over a 24-month window that differs between a positive
   and its matched negative only by the two cutoffs, so both get the same verdict by
   construction. All six provisional cases are RISKY under H. Under the C2 matching,
   H's precision is pinned near the base rate and its recall near 1.0; it is a floor,
   not a competitor, and §4 should say so.
2. **v2 admission works on real data.** On the six provisional bundles both title and
   description were admitted for every case; the pilot's 1-of-10 was the `updated_at`
   rule and cache age, not the data.
3. **The medium tier is the only large supply.** 244 medium-only PRs pass the fix-date
   gate. Admitting them makes the M4 human read the actual filter, with the
   `SAME_FUNCTION_FIX` attribution concern (M1) in full force.
