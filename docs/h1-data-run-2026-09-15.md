## Findings for the pre-registration

1. **Arm H cannot discriminate inside a subsystem-matched pair.** Its verdict is a
   property of the subsystem over a 24-month window that differs between a positive
   and its matched negative only by the two cutoffs, so both get the same verdict by
   construction. All six provisional cases are RISKY under H. Under the C2 matching,
   H's precision is pinned near the base rate and its recall near 1.0; it is a floor,
   not a competitor, and §4 should say so.
2. **v2 admission works on real data.** On the six provisional bundles both title and
   description were admitted for every case; the pilot's 1-of-10 was the `updated_at`
   rule and cache age, not the data.
3. **The medium tier is the only large supply.** 244 medium-only PRs pass the fix-date
   gate. Admitting them makes the M4 human read the actual filter, with the
   `SAME_FUNCTION_FIX` attribution concern (M1) in full force.

## Follow-on, 2026-09-16

The evaluator's read of these results changed the sampler (`73eb294`): subsystem
keying is now code-first, the matching key is selectable, positives can be capped per
subsystem, and a cohort may draw on more than one collection window. On the 2026H1
window alone, code-first keying plus a `category,subsystem` key takes the yield from
3 matched pairs to **10**; six positives still find no negative in their cell. The
2024 window is being re-correlated on the counting build to supply the rest.

## 2024 window re-correlated, 2026-09-16

The pilot's 2024 collection (1,667 PRs, `observation_end` 2026-08-21) was re-correlated
on the counting build so its records carry `correlated_by` and an uninspected-commit
count, which the sampler requires before it will accept a record as a negative. The
pilot's own mirror and heuristics configuration were used, read-only, at the same
observation end, so `config_hash`, `target_repo_head_sha` and `observation_end` are
unchanged; nothing was written into the pilot tree.

**The re-correlation reproduced the pilot's evidence exactly**: 1,667 of 1,667 records
unchanged, 0 flipped, 0 of 360 zero-signal records gained evidence, 0 uninspected commit
diffs. The 2024 data therefore has no timeout artefacts, and nothing in that half of the
cohort rests on a difference between the pilot build and the current one.

Supply, simulated over both windows with the relaxed `category,subsystem` key: 49 pairs
uncapped, **32 at `--max-per-subsystem 8`**, from 617 counted-clean negatives across 52
cells. The pre-registered 20/20 is reachable; the quota, not the supply, is now what
sets the cohort's subsystem profile, since `bgpd` holds 34 of the 55 2024 strong
candidates.
